Paragraph Frame for HW 1

Goal: Write a 6-sentence paragraph about your routine and what you are doing this month.
1

Read the model3 minutes

I'm Lucia, and I'm a student at a language school. On weekdays, I usually get up at 6:30 and take the bus to class. I have lunch with my friends at noon, and I study in the library every afternoon. On weekends, I often visit my grandmother. This month, I'm preparing for an English exam, so I'm studying more than usual. At the moment, I'm sitting in the library, and I want a cup of coffee.

Answer with a partner:

1Which sentences are about routines?
2Which sentences are about this month or right now?
3Find one verb that we do not usually use in the continuous.
4Find the time expressions. Which ones go with routines? Which ones go with now?

Useful time expressions

  • Routines: usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, on weekdays, on weekends
  • Now or temporary: now, at the moment, this month, this week, these days
Challenge (optional): Add a seventh sentence about a family member or friend. Use but to show a contrast. Example: My sister works in a bank, but this month she is taking a course.
